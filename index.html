01 Thesis

The next decade of enduring companies will be built on top of AI, not bolted onto it.

Every software business we’ve seen in the last eighteen months falls into one of two buckets: AI stapled onto a pre-existing P&L, or AI as the reason the business can exist at all. We invest, operate, and build exclusively in the second bucket.

01

Build

We incubate companies from a blank document. A small founding team, a sharp wedge into a real industry, and the AI leverage to ship what used to require an army.

02

Buy

We acquire profitable, under-loved software companies where modern AI can change the cost structure or the product surface — and we become the patient home that legacy funds can’t offer.

03

Hold

We don’t flip. Our capital is permanent, our horizon is generational, and our scoreboard is compounded free cash flow — not the next markup.

02 Approach

A holding company that actually operates.

We sit somewhere between a studio, a private-equity firm, and a long-only investor — and we are unapologetic about not fitting neatly into any of those boxes.

What we are

  • An operating company with permanent capital.
  • Founder-led and founder-friendly. We’ve sat in the chair.
  • Deeply technical. We ship prompts, evals, and product — not decks.
  • Concentrated. A handful of companies, owned for the long run.

What we are not

  • A venture fund with a ten-year clock.
  • A roll-up optimizing for exit multiples.
  • Tourists in AI who learned the words last quarter.
  • Passive. We work inside the companies we own.
The best companies of the next cycle will be ones that couldn’t have existed before — because the math didn’t work. We own the math.
